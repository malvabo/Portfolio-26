Food supply chain compliance

Automating compliance questionnaires and the evidence behind them

Turned a parsing brief into a complete work artifact, so a compliance team gets back the counterparty’s own document, answered and ready to send.

Role

Product and design lead

Scope

Research, product direction, design system

Timeframe

~8 weeks

Reach

5 customer teams and their suppliers

Impact summary

  • •Reframed a document-parsing brief into a finished deliverable
  • •Closed the loop from questionnaire to completed, correctly formatted document
  • •Built evidence evaluation as a shared service the platform’s other agents call
  • •Defined the product’s first design system and accessibility baseline

The problem & context

Companies moving food across a supply chain answer the same request constantly. A counterparty evaluating a shipment or a supplier sends a spreadsheet or a PDF with dozens of questions about the produce and the controls around it: origin and traceability, certification status, storage and handling, residue testing, the paperwork behind each claim.

  • 1.Answers were scattered across certification reports, internal policies and previously answered questionnaires
  • 2.Every counterparty sent a different format, so nothing could be reused as sent
  • 3.Supporting evidence arrived incomplete, and the gap was only found downstream

User needs

Research across both sides of the chain showed the time went into locating evidence rather than deciding anything. Compliance teams did not need a faster way to look things up. They needed the counterparty’s document back, completed, in the format it arrived in.

Business goal

Make the compliance workflow repeatable enough to scale across trade lanes without modelling each one first, so the company could widen the services it sold without adding headcount to every new customer.

Questionnaire workspace

Source questions, generated answers, and the evidence each answer came from

Approach

The strategy was to move the finish line. Extraction was treated as a step rather than the product, and everything downstream of it was designed so the customer ends up holding something they can send. To accomplish this:

  • 1.Mapped the document journey end to end, from evidence capture to the counterparty’s inbox
  • 2.Interviewed both sides of the chain, reaching hard-to-access suppliers through in-country consultants
  • 3.Made the deliverable the completed document, not a list of generated answers
  • 4.Moved the extraction schema into the customer’s hands, suggested from a sample
  • 5.Built evidence evaluation as a service the platform’s other agents call
  • 6.Defined status as the design system’s load-bearing primitive, with accessibility setting the constraints

Strategic moment: making the deliverable the document

The risk

Generated answers still left teams copying and pasting into whatever format had arrived that week.

The controversy

The team wanted to ship extraction and move on. Writing answers back into the source file extended scope late.

What changed after

Upload the questionnaire, review the answers, download a completed file with its original formatting intact.

Before: answers as a list to transcribe
After: the counterparty’s document, completed

Evidence evaluation infused into the platform

The second capability validates every piece of compliance evidence uploaded, checking captured screenshots for completeness, verifying formatting, and confirming nothing is missing before the evidence reaches a counterparty or an auditor.

It is not only customer-facing. A web agent captures evidence and sends it to the evaluator, which identifies the gaps and feeds them back so the agent can iterate until the evidence is complete. It gives the platform’s other agents a feedback loop to work against.

Impact

30+

Kickoff to a shipped product

5 teams

Customer teams onboarded

The product launched without the instrumentation to support an accuracy figure, so none is claimed here. The metric worth tracking is questionnaires completed without manual rework.

Closing insight

Automating expert work is not about extracting the data. It is about producing the artifact the other side actually asked for, and being able to show the evidence standing behind every line of it.
